laundry management system 
Laundry Service Request Flow
1. Provider Selection
1.1 Student selects a laundry provider based on:
• Location
• Price
• Service duration (1 day / 2 days)
2. Student Details Submission
2.1 Student enters the following details:
• Name
• Contact details
3. Clothes Category Selection
3.1 Student selects the type of clothes:
• Regular / Daily wear
• Delicate clothes
• Heavy clothes
• Formal wear
• Other (sportswear, underwear, socks, etc.)
3.2 Student enters the number of clothes for each category.
3.3 Price is determined per cloth according to the category price set by the laundry owner.
4. Service Type Selection
4.1 Student selects the required service:
• Wash & Dry
• Iron
• Other available services
4.2 Each service type has a predefined price.
5. Service Time Selection
5.1 Student selects the service duration:
• 12-hour service
• 24-hour service
• 48-hour service
• Weekly service
5.2 Each service option has a specific price.
6. Collection Notice
6.1 The system displays a message stating that clothes must be collected on the selected
date.
6.2 The laundry service will not be responsible for clothes after the collection date.
7. Price Calculation
7.1 The system automatically calculates the total price based on:
• Clothes category and quantity
• Selected service type
• Selected service duration
8. Request Submission
8.1 Student submits the request.
8.2 System displays the message: “You have successfully placed a request.”
8.3 System generates a unique Order Number.
9. Initial Order Status
9.1 Dashboard displays:
• Order Number
• Status: Successfully Requested
9.2 The system shows a message that clothes must be handed over to the laundry to
confirm the order.
10. Clothes Handover
10.1 The student physically hands over the clothes to the laundry.
11. Order Confirmation
11.1 Laundry confirms the order in the system.
11.2 Order status updates to Order Confirmed.
12. Service Processing
12.1 Laundry performs the selected services (wash, dry, iron, etc.).
13. Clothes Collection
13.1 Clothes are returned to the student on the selected date.
14. Payment and Completion
14.1 Student makes the payment.
14.2 System updates the order status to Order Complete.



Student Food Assistance System.

This system is specially designed to help university students who cannot go outside to buy food. Through this platform, students can request meals, and nearby students can help deliver them.

Now I will explain the process step by step.

First, Food Request Creation.
The student selects the food type such as Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner. Then they enter the required food items, quantity, and select the required time.

Second, Student Location and Details Submission.
The student enters their name, contact number, and current location such as hostel, boarding place, or university premises.

Third, Nearby Helper Notification.
The system automatically sends notifications to nearby students or helpers. They can see the request details including food type, location, and required time.

Fourth, Helper Acceptance.
A nearby student accepts the request. Once accepted, the contact details are shared between the requester and the helper.

Fifth, Food Purchasing.
The helper buys the requested food from the selected shop or university canteen.

Sixth, Delivery to the Requesting Student.
The helper delivers the food to the student’s location. The student verifies the order.

Seventh, Payment Process.
The student can pay by cash or online transfer. A small service charge can be included if necessary.

Finally, Order Completion.
The request is marked as completed in the system. The student can also provide ratings and feedback.

Only registered campus students can log in and access the marketplace.                                                                                                                       Next, the backend will support item posting functionality. Students will be able to upload item details including item name, description, price, category, and images. Each item will be linked to the student who posted it.

The system will also provide browse and search features. Students can view all available items and filter them by category to easily find what they need.

The backend will also handle the item details view, where users can see complete information about a product such as images, description, price, and seller contact details.

A reservation system will be implemented so that students can reserve an item before purchasing it. The item status will change from Available → Reserved → Sold, preventing multiple students from reserving the same item.

Finally, after the buyer and seller meet physically to exchange the item, the backend will record the completed transaction and close the listing in the system.
